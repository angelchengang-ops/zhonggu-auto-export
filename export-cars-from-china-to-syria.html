Check new-car eligibility before selecting stock

The Syrian Ministry of Economy and Industry explained its restriction on used-car imports on 30 June 2025 (official news source). Prioritize genuinely new, unused passenger vehicles, and ask a licensed Syrian customs representative to verify the latest applicable rules for the exact production date and unit before paying.

Send the model year, actual production date, VIN or identifiable stock reference, fuel type and specification sheet to your Syrian importing agent. We cannot promise that every China-market version can be cleared or registered in Syria.

Latakia, Tartus and a written shipping scope

Tell us whether your intended port is Latakia or Tartus, your final city, requested number of vehicles and shipment window. Freight, sailing acceptance, cargo insurance, destination charges and inland transport must be quoted and confirmed separately.

Do not assume that a port quote includes Syrian customs clearance, taxes, delivery to Damascus or Aleppo, or a guaranteed transit time.

What a dealer should include in the request

Please provide model/trim, quantity, target FOB budget, preferred China departure port, destination port, required documents, purchase schedule and preferred payment arrangements. We will respond with availability and quotation conditions after checking stock and compliance.

Commercial transactions remain subject to applicable sanctions screening, banking, customs and shipping-provider acceptance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I order used passenger cars for Syria?

General used passenger-car imports were restricted in June 2025. Ask a Syrian licensed importer to confirm current rules and any legally applicable exceptions before considering any used unit.

Can I get a CIF quote to Latakia or Tartus?

You may request an assessment. Actual routing, insurer acceptance, port conditions and pricing need current confirmation; no sailing or landed price is guaranteed.

Are MG5 or Coolray units already approved for Syria?

No approval is implied. Request the exact VIN, specifications and production date, then confirm eligibility with your licensed local agent.
