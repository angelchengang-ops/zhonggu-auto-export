What to Check About the Battery

  • Confirm cell brand, battery chemistry and pack capacity for the exact version.
  • Request the battery label or model code when needed for procurement documents.
  • Separate CLTC test range from your expected real-world driving range.
  • Check the actual charging connector, charger, supply requirements and local charging compatibility.
  • Ask for written spare-parts and after-sales terms for your destination.

China to Djibouti: Define the Quotation Scope

For an Ethiopia order routed through Djibouti, request the vehicle price and named shipment terms in writing. A FOB quote and a CIF Djibouti quote cover different stages of the journey. Neither should be assumed to include delivery to Addis Ababa, Ethiopian clearance, taxes or registration unless those items are expressly included.

Ask for separate confirmation of sea freight, insurance where applicable, port charges, inland transport and local clearance. Shipment dates and freight costs are confirmed for the actual order; no fixed transit time or landed price is promised here.

Before Reserving Vehicles

Have your importer confirm current requirements for the exact vehicle and its documents. A pure-electric description alone is not proof of import or registration eligibility. Share any required document list before the order is agreed.

Provide the trim, quantity, destination city, requested port arrangement and target delivery date. Request current vehicle photos and a final equipment list, especially for optional screens, stability control or seating upgrades.
