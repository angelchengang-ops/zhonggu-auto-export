Name the port and the final delivery city

If you are considering a sea-freight quotation to Bandar Abbas, specify the receiving terminal, such as Shahid Rajaee, and the final city, for example Tehran, Isfahan or Shiraz. For a land route, provide the intended border entry point instead. A named destination is a request for a feasibility and price check, not confirmation of an available route.

Separate vehicle, freight, insurance, destination handling and onward transport in your cost comparison. Ask the carrier and receiving agent to confirm acceptance, responsibilities, validity and current scheduling. See the UNECE listing for Bandar Shahid Rajaee for location identification, not shipment authorization.

Prepare a quotation that can be checked

  1. Send buyer type, model/trim, condition, quantity and the preferred purchase date.
  2. Identify the departure port if known, Iranian entry point and final delivery city.
  3. Specify the budget currency explicitly. Do not mix USD, rial and toman amounts or treat a currency conversion as an agreed selling price.
  4. Ask for the vehicle specification, available inspection material, document responsibilities, itemized quotation and validity period.
  5. Before any payment or booking, confirm that the parties, payment arrangements and carrier service comply with applicable trade restrictions and approvals.

Any quotation remains subject to vehicle availability and the required commercial and compliance review. No tax rate, approval deadline or fixed delivery time is advertised here.

Iran Vehicle Sourcing Questions

Is the displayed FOB price the total cost in Iran?

No. The listed Full Option FOB amount is a China-side vehicle quotation reference. Ask for a named port and written scope; freight, insurance, destination charges, taxes and onward transport need separate confirmation.

Can I enquire without opening WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the form on this page and provide your contact number and, if useful, an email address. Opening a messaging application is not required to send the website form.

Can every model in the catalogue be imported into Iran?

No blanket eligibility is implied. Your representative must confirm the applicable buyer route and the exact vehicle before you reserve stock or arrange payment.

What is needed for a Bandar Abbas quotation?

Provide the exact model and trim, quantity, intended terminal, final delivery city and timing. Current carrier acceptance, route, commercial terms and compliance requirements must be reviewed before booking.
