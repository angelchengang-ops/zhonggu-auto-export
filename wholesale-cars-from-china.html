Frequently Asked Questions

How do I receive the current wholesale stock list?

Send your destination, preferred models and target quantity through the inquiry form or WhatsApp. Current stock is checked before a quotation is issued.

Are wholesale prices fixed?

No. Public prices are reference FOB prices where shown. Final quotations depend on current inventory, quantity, vehicle details, loading port and destination requirements.

Can one quotation include several models?

Yes. Importers can request a mixed sourcing plan. Final loading and shipping options depend on vehicle dimensions, locations and current logistics conditions.

Which China supply locations are available?

Current vehicle resources may be located in Qingdao, at Nansha Port or in Horgos. The actual location is confirmed for each vehicle before order.
