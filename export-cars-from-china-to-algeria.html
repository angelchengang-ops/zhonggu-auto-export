FAQ for Algeria Car Importers

Do you have Geely Coolray ready stock for Algeria?

We can check Geely Coolray Full Option ready stock at Nansha Port. Colors, quantity and VIN information are confirmed during inquiry.

Can Algeria buyers request used Bestune B70 batch stock?

Buyers can request details of China-side B70 2021-2023 listings from Qingdao. This is not a statement that every model year or unit can be imported into Algeria. Confirm registration documents and current eligibility before choosing units.

Do you guarantee local import approval?

No. Buyers must confirm current Algeria import, tax, certification and registration rules before order.

What should I send for a quotation to Algiers?

Send the exact model and trim, new or used condition, quantity, destination port and purchasing timetable. For a used vehicle, also specify acceptable mileage and request its registration documents.

Is a CIF quotation a fixed all-in landed price?

Do not assume that it is. Ask for an itemized written quotation that identifies the destination port, freight and insurance items, validity and exclusions. Confirm destination clearance, taxes, port charges and inland delivery separately.
