Know the Battery You Are Buying

This version lists CATL cells with LFP chemistry and 27.19 kWh pack capacity. Brand, chemistry and capacity describe different aspects of the battery; they are not a cell or pack model code.

For an order requiring a specific battery model, request the battery label and vehicle configuration record for the offered unit. No unverified cell or pack code is stated here.

Range and Charging in Everyday Use

CLTC range is a laboratory test figure. Actual range depends on speed, load, terrain, weather and air-conditioning use. Charging times are reference figures and depend on charger output, battery temperature and state of charge.

Confirm the charging connector, AC supply requirements and included charger before ordering. Do not assume charging hardware or domestic warranty terms transfer unchanged to your destination.
