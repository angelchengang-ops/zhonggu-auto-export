Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the China car importer center designed for?

It is designed for overseas car importers, dealers, trading companies, rental fleets and other B2B buyers sourcing new and used vehicles from China.

Can Zhonggu Auto Export prepare both FOB and CIF quotations?

Yes. FOB quotations can be prepared after stock confirmation. CIF quotations also require the destination port and a current shipping-cost check.

What information should an importer send?

Send the destination country and port, preferred models, target quantity, budget and purchasing schedule so the team can prepare a relevant stock and quotation plan.

Can I request VINs, photos and port videos?

Available VIN information, current vehicle photos and port videos can be provided for confirmed stock when those materials are available.

Do you supply both new and used vehicles?

Yes. Zhonggu Auto Export handles selected new vehicles, ready stock and inspected used-car inventory for export inquiries.
