Questions About the Five-Door Xiaoma

Is this the four-door or five-door Xiaoma?

It has four passenger doors plus a rear hatch, so the body style is called five-door. There are four seats. This is separate from the original three-door Xiaoma.

Which battery does the five-door Xiaoma use?

All four listed trims use CATL lithium iron phosphate (LFP) cells. The 222 km CLTC versions list 16.97 kWh; the 328 km versions list 27.19 kWh. These figures are capacities, not battery model codes.

Can you confirm an exact battery model code?

Request the battery label and vehicle configuration record for the offered unit. We do not publish an unverified cell or battery-pack model code.

How much does it cost to export?

All four trims are price on request. Send your trim, quantity and destination for an itemized quotation. Domestic retail prices are not export or landed prices.

Are the photos specific to each trim?

The photos show an actual five-door showroom vehicle. They illustrate the model family; equipment and accessories may differ by trim. Confirm the actual units before ordering.
