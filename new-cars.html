Why Choose Zhonggu Auto Export

Zhonggu Auto Export helps overseas dealers compare vehicle options, request FOB quotations, prepare export documents and coordinate shipping communication from China.

Our sourcing support covers multi-brand dealer network resources, selected used vehicles, new car options and practical export preparation for B2B buyers.

Available Vehicles or Brands

Buyers can request BYD, Geely, Bestune, Hongqi, GAC and other Chinese new car options, as well as selected Toyota, Honda and Volkswagen used vehicles.

Export Process

  1. Confirm target model, quantity and destination.
  2. Prepare FOB quotation and available stock list.
  3. Coordinate inspection, documents and export preparation.
  4. Arrange customs declaration and shipping coordination.
